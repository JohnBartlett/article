The Music Institute Honors Chicago Legends May 18

By Judy Carmack Bross — For May 3, 2026

Bill Kurtis and Donna La Pietra receive the Cultural Visionary Award; the Lyric Opera of Chicago receives the Dushkin Award at the Music Institute of Chicago’s 95th Anniversary Gala, Monday May 18 at the Four Seasons Hotel Chicago. Grammy Award–winning Marta Aznavoorian receives the Richard D. Colburn Award for Teaching Excellence. Gala co-chairs: Carlos R. Cardenas and Shiraz Kotte.

Chicago: An Express History Part Two — Riding the Gravy Train

By Jackson LeJeune (via Ana Baca) — For May 3, 2026

Part 2 of 3: Chicago 1871–1939. Covers the Great Fire, the rise of the L, the 1893 World’s Fair, meatpacking dominance, labor struggles (Haymarket, Pullman Strike), the Great Migration, Prohibition, and the Great Depression’s impact on the city. Teaser: “By 1900, Chicago had become the second city of the nation, teeming with people and built on the bedrock of its railroad network.”

Preservation Foundation Continues to Embrace Its Mission 50 Years After Launching

By David A. F. Sweet (Unsung Gems) — For May 3, 2026

Lake Forest Preservation Foundation marks its 50th anniversary under new Executive Director Jennifer McGregor. Covers cemetery headstone inventory, Lake Forest Library renovation, new Adrian Smith Preservationist Award, digitization of 50 years of archives, and upcoming celebrations (Bank Lane block party May 3, black-and-white gala June 26, exhibit Aug. 20, symposium Oct. 16–18 with Geoffrey Baer). First appeared in Lake Forest Love.

Full article text received Apr 29 from Judy. Three photo attachments: (1) Lasker Estate Garage House 2025 event — possible cover; (2) Jennifer McGregor in front of restored David Adler house on Albert Lasker estate; (3) same Lasker Estate Garage House event photo.
