Field Museum Special Event: Pokémon Fossil Museum

By Adrian Naves  •  Adrian’s Adventures — CCM Intern

Chicago is the only city in North America where fans can experience the Pokémon Fossil Museum — a one-of-a-kind exhibition opening at the Field Museum on May 22, 2026. Ancient Pokémon like Tyrantrum and Archeops meet real fossils including Sue the T. Rex and the Chicago Archaeopteryx. Features three Field Museum scientists.
