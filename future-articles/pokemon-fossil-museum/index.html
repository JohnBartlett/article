Field Museum Special Event: Pokémon Fossil Museum

Chicago is the only city in North America where fans can experience the Pokémon Fossil Museum — a one-of-a-kind exhibition opening at the Field Museum in May 2026.

By Adrian Naves

CCM Intern

This upcoming spring, Chicago is getting a special treat — the Field Museum is going to have fossils to discover! These aren’t just any kind of fossils, they’re Pokémon fossils!

The Field Museum is going to host a special event when The Pokémon Company International will make its North America debut in Chicago for the Pokémon Fossil Museum exhibition. The exhibit will open on May 22, 2026, and will run until April 11, 2027. Chicago is the only city in North America where fans can visit the event.

The exhibition first opened in Japan and was created in collaboration with The National Museum of Nature and Science in Tokyo and The Pokémon Company.

The World of Pokémon

In the Pokémon world, the main goal is to become a trainer — and becoming the best Pokémon trainer in the world means battling other trainers and obtaining gym badges, eventually qualifying in tournaments and earning the ability to battle the champion. The real joy of Pokémon is capturing whichever Pokémon you feel like catching to help you achieve that goal.

You catch a Pokémon in the tall grass, trees, water, or air. One of the most fun and unique methods of catching a Pokémon is by obtaining a Pokémon fossil. Once you get a fossil, you can revive an ancient Pokémon and add them to your collection — which is precisely what this special exhibit will help explain, with real-world context and comparison.

Ancient Pokémon Meets Real Paleontology

The Pokémon Fossil Museum exhibition will invite visitors to compare fan-favorite ancient Pokémon like Tyrantrum and Archeops with actual fossils from the Field Museum’s collection — including the world-famous Sue the T. Rex and the Chicago Archaeopteryx. The interactive exhibits will showcase the beloved Pokémon as a gateway to paleontology, evolution, and natural history.

According to the Field Museum website: “Three Field Museum scientists are featured throughout the exhibition: Chief Fossil Preparator Akiko Shinya, Assistant Curator of Early Tetrapods Arjan Mann, and Associate Curator of Fossil Reptiles Jingmai O’Connor. Their research and personal stories are woven into the experience, grounding the fantasy in real careers and real discoveries.”

The Pokémon Fossil Museum will hopefully inspire visitors to make new discoveries about some of their favorite Pokémon and offer a pathway to science and paleontology. This is a must-visit for Pokémon fans new and old, plus fans of paleontology!

Exhibition Details

Pokémon Fossil Museum at the Field Museum
Opens: May 22, 2026
Closes: April 11, 2027
Chicago is the only North American venue
For more information: fieldmuseum.org/exhibition/pokemon

“Chicago is the only city in North America where fans can visit the Pokémon Fossil Museum — the Chicago-only North American debut of a world-class exhibition.”
