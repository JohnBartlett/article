How to read this — and what it can’t do. GA4 reports traffic in aggregate, and there is no shared key between a submitted comment and a specific browsing session. Per-visitor attribution would require the GA4 → BigQuery export (not enabled) and would still be blocked by GA4’s small-count privacy thresholds. So no row below identifies who left a comment. The “GA4 signal” column is the article’s own traffic today plus the site-wide reader backdrop; the “Reasonably inferred” column is reasoning from the comment’s wording, any self-provided identity, its timestamp, and those aggregate patterns — labeled by confidence. Treat inferences as hunches, not facts.
