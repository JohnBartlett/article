Children's Research Fund Children's Ball

February 15, 2026 • By Judy Carmack Bross

The Children's Research Fund's Children's Ball made a triumphant return to the Hilton Chicago last month, drawing more than 1,000 guests to its black-tie gala. Co-chaired by Jennifer and Gerald Beeson alongside Jenni Sorenson and John Holmes, the elegant evening of dinner and dancing raised an impressive $2.2 million, pushing the event's cumulative fundraising total to $5.6 million. The remarkable showing marks a record-breaking year for the prestigious annual fundraiser.

This year's gala embraced the theme "Be the Spark," celebrating research's transformative power to ignite breakthrough ideas in pediatric medicine. The evening's program featured a particularly moving moment: a young patient who had been cured of sickle cell disease through a clinical trial at Lurie Children's Hospital. This living testament to the impact of research funding underscored the vital importance of the Children's Ball's mission and the real-world difference that donor support makes in advancing life-saving treatments for children.
