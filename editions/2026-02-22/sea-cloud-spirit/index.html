Sea Cloud Spirit

February 22, 2026 • By Nick Wilder

Sailing has always been in my blood. As a grade schooler I learned to sail in dinghys on the Maine coast. One summer I taught kids to sail in Cold Spring Harbor on Long Island Sound. In college I was the Radcliffe sailing instructor. Nowadays my wife, Keven, and I enjoy sailing our Alerion 28 off Sister Bay in Door County. A few years ago we discovered the pleasures of sailing on the Sea Cloud schooners. Our first schooner cruise was around the Baltic from Helsinki to Copenhagen. In 2024 we sailed through the Greek islands from Athens to Istanbul. In January of this year we boarded the Sea Cloud Spirit in Sint Maarten for a week of sailing through the British Virgin Islands. It does my heart good when the captain turns off the engine and raises the sails en route to another tropical isle.
