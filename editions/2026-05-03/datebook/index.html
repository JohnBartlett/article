Chicago Opera Theater
Saturday, May 30 3:00 p.m.

Trusted — Concert World Premiere

Chicago Opera Theater (COT) closes its 2025/26 season with the concert premiere of a new opera Trusted — the seventh full-length opera developed through COT’s Vanguard Initiative. Composed by Aaron Israel Levin to a libretto by Marella Martin Koch, Trusted was developed over the course of Levin’s two year tenure as COT’s Vanguard Composer. Audiences were invited to view a piano-vocal workshop of the opera earlier this season; now they can see how the piece has developed in its fully orchestrated premiere.

The Studebaker Theater at the Fine Arts Building
410 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago
