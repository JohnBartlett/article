The Goodman Theatre
Now – December 27

Theater of the Mind

Co-created by Academy, Grammy and Tony Award-winning artist David Byrne and writer Mala Gaonkar, and presented by The Goodman, Theater of the Mind is a new theatrical experience you’ll see, feel, taste and hear. Inspired by both historical and current neuroscience research, the show takes you on an intimate and immersive journey inside how we see and create our worlds. Peer behind the curtain of the physical realm as you move through a series of rooms with your guide, where you’ll participate in thought-provoking experiences—and marvel at the wonders of your mind.

Reid Murdoch Building
333 North LaSalle Drive, Chicago

Elmhurst Art Museum
November 5 – 8

Exhibition A Revolution of Values to be presented as part of third Veteran Art Triennial and Summit, Resisting the Long War

The Elmhurst Art Museum is honored to announce the participating artists and related programming for A REVOLUTION OF VALUES, a group exhibition presented as part of the 2026 Veteran Art Triennial and Summit, RESISTING THE LONG WAR, organized by the Chicago-based Emerging Veteran Art Movement. A REVOLUTION OF VALUES builds on the legacy ofVietnam Veterans Against the War through the work of cutting-edge contemporary veteran artists who are turning from militarism toward solidarity. Bringing together nearly a dozen local, national, and international artists, the exhibition celebrates the range of ways veteran artists examine their military experiences and critique militarism. A REVOLUTION OF VALUES will be on view from September 12, 2026 to January 3, 2027.

Elmhurst Art Museum
150 South Cottage Hill Avenue, Elmhurst

Paul Marinaro Presents Mood Ellington

Hey Nonny will pay tribute to one of the most influential figures in American music, Duke Ellington, with a one-night-only performance marking the 80th anniversary of his historic 1946 concert at Chicago's Civic Opera House. Acclaimed Chicago jazz vocalist Paul Marinaro will present Mood Ellington, joined by a full nine-piece ensemble performing the timeless music of Ellington and Billy Strayhorn. The celebrated project was also featured at this year's Chicago Jazz Festival, making this an incredible opportunity to experience world-class jazz in an intimate setting. Tickets start at $38.

Guarneri Hall
Wednesday & Thursday, November 11 & 12 6:30 p.m.

Revolutions

Guarneri Hall, a state-of-the-art, custom-built classical music venue in the heart of downtown Chicago, proudly announces Revolutions, a two-day music series exploring the intersections of political, social, technological, and musical change at two pivotal moments in world history. Audiences will hear works by Mozart, Haydn, Beethoven, Ravel, Stravinsky and Janáček, paired with lectures that provide socio-political context of the eras. Adding to the intimacy, each musical evening includes a post-concert reception where audience members and performers can interact over quality libations and hors d’oeuvres.

Guarneri Hall
11 East Adams Street, Chicago
