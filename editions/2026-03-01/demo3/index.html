Astrological Forecast: March 2026

Daily Star by Victoria Martin · Times in Central Time (CST before March 8, CDT after)

About the Daily Star

Daily Star by Victoria Martin highlights the key mood or pattern to watch each day. Sometimes that “star” is metaphorical—a central theme or feeling. Other times, it echoes visible sky events or the fixed stars. Victoria is known in the Chicago community for her fixed‑star work and will occasionally weave those celestial landmarks into her daily guidance.

How to Read This Page

  • Daily Star: one‑sentence snapshot of the day.
  • Energy & Mood: what the day feels like in plain language.
  • Best Use of the Day: practical ways to work with it.
  • Technical Notes: astrological details for enthusiasts.

Sunday, March 1

Daily Star by Victoria Martin: Ease into the coming time change while Full Moon energy builds; people are edgy and convinced they’re right, so avoid needless clashes.
