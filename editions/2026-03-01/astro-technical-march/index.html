Shared Technical Frame
  • Coordinate system: Geocentric, tropical, apparent positions (Sun through Pluto).
  • Extra points: True North/South Node, Chiron, Ceres, Pallas, Juno, Vesta.
  • Sidereal: Subtract Lahiri ayanamsha (≈ 24° in 2026) from any tropical longitude for rough sidereal placement.
  • Houses: Whole-sign zodiacal (Aries = 1st … Pisces = 12th). Quadrant cusps (Placidus, Koch) require a specific moment and latitude; not generated here.
  • Orbs in use: Sun/Moon to planets ≤ 8°; planet–planet 4–5°; asteroids to planets/angles 2–3°.
  • Active retrogrades: Jupiter Rx in Cancer (throughout early March); Mercury Rx in Pisces (through March 20).
