Textiles at Fondazione Antonio Ratti

By Sigalina Zetouni · April 26, 2026

In Como, Italy, overlooking Lake Como, Fondazione Antonio Ratti (FAR) is located in Villa Sucota. On a visit in March of 2026, I saw several superb pieces from Antonio Ratti's antique textile collection that were exhibited with contemporary works in "Animal Stories," that is currently on view. It was magnificent to be surrounded by historical fabrics that featured animals, and understand the dialogue with contemporary art that has been focusing on the role of animals. The exhibition is on view until June 14, 2026.
