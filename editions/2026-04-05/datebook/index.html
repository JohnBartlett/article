April
Renaissance Society
Wednesday, April 8 7:00 – 10:00 p.m.

RenBen 2026

Shaped by the vision of Maurizio Cattelan—known for his subversive wit, conceptual audacity, and ability to surprise—RenBen is the Renaissance Society’s annual gala, raising a significant portion of the museum’s annual budget and enabling new artistic commissions, scholarly discourse, and lasting publications. This year, Cattelan’s “Silent Party” will transform two floors of the historic Chicago Athletic Association into a labyrinth.

Chicago Athletic Association
12 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago

Intuit Art Museum
Friday, April 10 5:30 p.m.

Life is an Art: IAM the Party

Intuit Art Museum (IAM) celebrates the first anniversary of its museum expansion with this “Life is an Art”-themed gala, where guests are invited to dress creatively and be the artwork. Timed to coincide with EXPO Art Week, the gala event is co-hosted by Kahil El’Zabar and Tony Karman and honors internationally celebrated artists Nick Cave and Bob Faust with the Trendsetter Award, and beloved Chicago art educator Jerry Stefl with the Visionary Award. IAM partners with the Museum of House and Gertie for the “Art After Dark Dance Party” featuring DJ Lady D and local celebrity DJs.

838 West Kinzie Street, Chicago

Get Lit: Speakeasy Rhymes

Step back into the glittering world of the Roaring Twenties at an evening of clandestine charm, jazz-age flair, and unfiltered artistic expression at the American Writers Museum. Stop by the Blackout Poetry Bar to create your own blackout poetry inspired by literary works of the 1920s. Get an original poem-on-demand from Poems While You Wait. Uncover the Secret Password hidden in clues around the museum to earn a special prize. Share an original poem or song—or recite a work from a notable American writer—at the Open Mic.
