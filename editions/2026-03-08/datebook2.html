Classic Chicago — March 8, 2026

DateBook

Galas, concerts, lectures, and celebrations — the essential guide to Chicago’s social season

Curated by Anne Delfosse
March
Equality Illinois
Saturday, March 13 6:00 – 11:00 p.m.

2026 Equality Illinois Gala

The state’s oldest and largest LGBTQ+ civil rights organization celebrates its annual gala, honoring community leaders and raising funds to support advocacy, education, and coalition-building efforts across Illinois. An evening of cocktails, dinner, awards, and entertainment bringing together advocates, allies, and supporters.

Hilton Chicago
720 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago
equalityillinois.org
Chicago Opera Theater
Friday, March 19 – Sunday, March 28

Spring Season Opening

Chicago Opera Theater opens its 2025/26 season with innovative productions featuring emerging and established artists. Known for presenting opera in English with contemporary staging, COT continues its mission to make opera accessible and relevant to diverse audiences.

Studebaker Theater
410 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago
chicagooperatheater.org
100 Club of Illinois
Friday, March 26 Noon – 2:00 p.m.

2026 First Look for Charity

The Chicago Auto Show’s premier charity event returns for its 32nd year, raising funds for 18 beneficiary organizations serving children, families, and individuals with special needs. VIP guests enjoy an exclusive first look at the latest vehicles, gourmet lunch, and champagne reception while supporting critical community programs. Since inception, First Look has raised over $67 million.

McCormick Place
2301 South King Drive, Chicago
100clubil.org/firstlook
April
Enlace Chicago
Saturday, April 4 6:00 – 10:00 p.m.

Annual Gala 2026

Enlace Chicago’s signature fundraising event celebrates the organization’s work empowering Latino youth and families in Little Village through education, health, and community development programs. The evening features dinner, live music, awards honoring community champions, and a spirited live auction supporting programming that serves over 10,000 annually.

Hilton Chicago
720 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago
enlacechicago.org/annualgala2026
Ignite Promise
Saturday, April 11 6:00 – 11:00 p.m.

Ignite & Shine Gala 2026

Celebrating 50 years of empowering youth through education, mentorship, and career development, Ignite Promise hosts its golden anniversary gala. Honoring five decades of impact, the evening supports scholarships and programs helping underserved students achieve their academic and professional dreams.

The Ritz-Carlton Chicago
160 East Pearson Street, Chicago
ignitepromise.org
Chicago Children’s Museum
Thursday, April 16 6:30 – 10:00 p.m.

Spring Gala 2026

Chicago Children’s Museum celebrates another year of sparking curiosity and creativity in young minds. This family-friendly fundraiser supports the museum’s commitment to providing accessible, hands-on learning experiences for all children, regardless of background or ability. Guests enjoy cocktails, dinner, and exclusive access to interactive exhibits.

Chicago Children’s Museum at Navy Pier
700 East Grand Avenue, Chicago
chicagochildrensmuseum.org/gala
Off the Street Club
Friday, April 24 6:00 – 10:00 p.m.

Spring Benefit

For over a century, Off The Street Club has provided West Side youth with safe spaces, mentorship, and opportunities. This annual benefit raises funds for after-school programs, summer camps, STEM education, and athletic leagues serving 500+ children daily. An evening celebrating young people’s achievements and the community’s investment in their futures.

Morgan Manufacturing
401 North Morgan Street, Chicago
offthestreetclub.com/events
Mercy Home for Boys & Girls
Saturday, April 25 6:00 p.m. – midnight

Evening of Hope Gala

Mercy Home’s signature fundraising gala supports residential care and therapeutic services for abused, neglected, and at-risk youth. Since 1887, Mercy Home has provided safe haven, education, and healing for Chicago’s most vulnerable children. The Evening of Hope features inspiring stories of transformation, a live auction, and elegant dinner honoring those who champion children in crisis.

Hilton Chicago
720 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago
mercyhome.org
May
Field Museum
Saturday, May 2 6:00 p.m. – midnight

2026 Spring Gala

One of Chicago’s most anticipated museum events, the Field Museum Gala transforms the iconic Stanley Field Hall into an elegant venue beneath SUE the T. rex. Guests explore world-class exhibitions, enjoy gourmet cuisine and cocktails, bid on unique auction items, and dance late into the night—all while supporting groundbreaking scientific research and educational programming reaching millions worldwide.

Field Museum
1400 South Lake Shore Drive, Chicago
fieldmuseum.org/gala
Chicago Humanities Festival
Throughout May

Spring Festival: Ideas That Matter

The Chicago Humanities Festival presents its spring programming featuring renowned authors, thinkers, journalists, and artists in conversations exploring the ideas shaping our world. From intimate salon-style discussions to major public lectures, the festival brings diverse perspectives to stages across the city.

Various Venues Throughout Chicago
chicagohumanities.org/events
Chicago Cultural Alliance
Saturday, May 15 5:00 – 9:00 p.m.

Cultural Connections Benefit

The Chicago Cultural Alliance celebrates the city’s rich tapestry of ethnic museums and cultural centers serving diverse communities. This benefit supports the Alliance’s work amplifying underrepresented cultural voices, building partnerships, and ensuring equitable access to arts and heritage resources across Chicago’s neighborhoods.

National Museum of Mexican Art
1852 West 19th Street, Chicago
chicagoculturalalliance.org
June
Lincoln Park Zoo
Saturday, June 6 6:30 – 11:00 p.m.

Zoo-ologie Cocktail Party

The Lincoln Park Zoo Auxiliary Board’s signature summer fundraiser transforms the historic Café Brauer into an elegant cocktail venue overlooking the zoo grounds. Guests sip craft cocktails, sample gourmet bites from Chicago’s finest restaurants, and enjoy sunset views of the lagoon while supporting free zoo admission, conservation programs, and animal care excellence.

Café Brauer, Lincoln Park Zoo
2021 North Stockton Drive, Chicago
lpzoo.org/zooologie
Chicago Botanic Garden
Saturday, June 13 6:00 p.m. – midnight

Summer Dinner Dance

The Chicago Botanic Garden’s Auxiliary hosts its annual black-tie gala amid the Garden’s breathtaking landscapes at peak bloom. Under elegant tents surrounded by roses and perennials, guests enjoy cocktails, fine dining, live music, and dancing beneath the stars. Proceeds support horticultural research, education programs, and conservation initiatives at one of the world’s premier living museums.

Chicago Botanic Garden
1000 Lake Cook Road, Glencoe
chicagobotanic.org/dinnerParty
National Hellenic Museum
Thursday, June 18 6:30 – 10:00 p.m.

51st Anniversary Gala

The National Hellenic Museum celebrates Greek heritage and contemporary Hellenic culture with its annual gala honoring individuals whose leadership exemplifies Greek values of excellence, service, and philanthropy. The evening features authentic Greek cuisine, wine, entertainment, and awards supporting the museum’s mission to share Greek history and contributions with the American public.

National Hellenic Museum
333 South Halsted Street, Chicago
nationalhellenicmuseum.org
July
Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago
Saturday, July 11 7:00 p.m. – 1:00 a.m.

Summer Gala & Artist Ball

MCA Chicago’s legendary summer gala brings together artists, collectors, curators, and contemporary art enthusiasts for an unforgettable evening of performance, installation, and celebration. Each year’s event features commissioned works by leading contemporary artists, transforming the museum into an immersive artistic experience while raising essential funds for groundbreaking exhibitions and educational programs.

Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago
220 East Chicago Avenue, Chicago
mcachicago.org
Art Institute of Chicago
Thursday, July 23 6:30 – 11:00 p.m.

Modern Wing Garden Party

Set in the stunning Modern Wing’s outdoor sculpture terrace, this exclusive summer soirée celebrates the Art Institute’s world-renowned collection and commitment to artistic excellence. Against the backdrop of Millennium Park and Chicago’s skyline, guests enjoy cocktails, gourmet fare, live music, and special exhibition access while supporting one of the world’s greatest art museums.

Art Institute of Chicago
111 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago
artic.edu/events
August
Griffin Museum of Science and Industry
Saturday, August 1 7:00 p.m. – midnight

Black Creativity Gala

The Griffin Museum of Science and Industry’s signature Black Creativity Gala celebrates innovation, achievement, and excellence in the Black community. This black-tie celebration features live entertainment, immersive exhibits, gourmet cuisine, and awards honoring leaders advancing science, technology, engineering, and mathematics education and access.

Griffin Museum of Science and Industry
5700 South DuSable Lake Shore Drive, Chicago
griffinmsi.org/blackcreativity
Diversity Scholarship Foundation
Friday, August 21 6:00 – 11:00 p.m.

Unity Gala

The Diversity Scholarship Foundation’s Unity Gala brings together business leaders, educators, and advocates supporting diversity in law and professional services. Since 2004, DSF has awarded over $3 million in scholarships to students of color pursuing legal careers. The gala celebrates scholarship recipients’ achievements and raises funds ensuring the next generation of diverse legal leaders.

Ritz-Carlton Chicago
160 East Pearson Street, Chicago
diversitychicago.org
September
Chicago Symphony Orchestra
Throughout September 2026 – May 2027

2026/27 Season at Symphony Center

The Chicago Symphony Orchestra launches its 136th season with conductor Riccardo Muti leading performances of masterworks spanning four centuries. From Beethoven and Brahms to contemporary premieres, the CSO continues its tradition of artistic excellence with performances featuring world-renowned soloists and conductors. Special gala opening night celebration honors patrons and launches a season of unforgettable musical experiences.

Symphony Center
220 South Michigan Avenue, Chicago
cso.org
Lyric Opera of Chicago
September 2026 – May 2027

2026/27 Season

Lyric Opera of Chicago presents its season of grand opera featuring international stars, spectacular productions, and beloved classics alongside bold new works. Opening night gala celebrates Lyric’s commitment to making opera accessible and relevant to diverse audiences. From Wagner to contemporary commissions, Lyric’s season exemplifies the power of opera to move, inspire, and unite.

Lyric Opera House
20 North Wacker Drive, Chicago
lyricopera.org
Chicago Summer Opera
Throughout Summer 2026

Summer Festival Season

Chicago Summer Opera presents emerging artists in intimate productions showcasing the next generation of operatic talent. Productions feature fully staged operas with orchestra, performed by young professionals in smaller venues allowing audiences unprecedented proximity to performers. A celebration of artistry, training, and the future of opera in Chicago.

Various Venues, Chicago
chicagosummeropera.org
