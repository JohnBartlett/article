Chicago Architecture Center
October 17–18, 2026

Open House Chicago 2026

Chicago’s beloved Open House Chicago returns for a landmark weekend, throwing open the doors of hundreds of extraordinary buildings — historic landmarks, private residences, architectural gems, and active workplaces — that are rarely, if ever, accessible to the public. Organized by the Chicago Architecture Center, this free annual festival transforms the city into an open-air museum, drawing tens of thousands of curious Chicagoans into the hidden interiors that define one of the world’s great architectural cities.

Buildings Throughout Chicago
