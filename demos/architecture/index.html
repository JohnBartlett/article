The grand foyer and the living room’s Palmer-estate marble columns. Listing photographs © VHT Studios for @properties, shown for review.

1301 North Astor Street

Philip B. Maher designed this eleven-residence Art Deco cooperative at Astor and Goethe in 1928. Inside, David Adler created a two-level home for the Potter Palmer family, and it has had only three owners since.

A 19-foot foyer with a historically designated curved staircase, Aegean marble columns from the Palmer estate, and woodwork from Ulysses S. Grant’s suite at the Palmer House.

8 bedrooms · 6 full, 1 half baths · about 7,000 sq ft · co-op · coming soon at $2,500,000
