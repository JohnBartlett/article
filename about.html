About Classic Chicago

The city's premier Sunday magazine

Classic Chicago Magazine is a weekly digital publication celebrating the people, places, culture, and style that make Chicago one of the world's great cities. Published every Sunday, each edition brings together original writing, photography, and reporting from voices who know and love this city.

Our Mission

We believe Chicago deserves a magazine that reflects its depth — its world-class dining, its storied neighborhoods, its vibrant arts scene, and the remarkable people who call it home. Classic Chicago tells the stories that matter to those who care deeply about this city, past and present.

What We Cover

From the best restaurants on Halsted Street to hidden gardens in Wicker Park, from galas on the Gold Coast to boba tea in Chinatown — our writers explore every corner of Chicago life. Regular features include dining guides, social coverage, neighborhood profiles, travel writing, and cultural commentary.

Our Writers This Week

Dr. Robert Leo Murphy

Chicago–Paris Connection

A physician and longtime Francophile, Dr. Robert Leo Murphy writes Classic Chicago’s “Chicago–Paris Connection” series, profiling Chicagoans who have built remarkable careers in Europe. He lives in Paris’s 7th arrondissement.

Sydney Armstrong

Cultural Heritage

Sydney Armstrong writes about Chicago’s cultural institutions and the communities that sustain them. This week she profiles the National Museum of Mexican Art in Pilsen.

Kipper Hendrick

Life & Family

Kipper Hendrick is a caregiver advocate and consultant based in Palm Beach. After more than two decades helping care for aging parents and loved ones, she founded Lance Consulting to guide families through the often complex medical, emotional, and logistical challenges of caregiving. Through her writing and social media platforms (@CareWithKipper), she shares practical tools, encouragement, and real-life insights to support long-haul caregivers.

Jill Lowe

Facts & Froth

Under the banner of “Facts and Froth,” Jill Lowe writes about the quirky and curious aspects of the ordinary and familiar parts of our everyday life. Photo of Jill by Joe Mazza, Bravelux Inc.

Darcy L. Evon

Arts & Culture

Darcy L. Evon serves on the Guild of the Chicago History Museum Board of Directors. She is a nonprofit executive, book author, journalist, and former columnist for the Chicago Sun-Times and magazine publisher.

Jen Huang

CCM Intern

Jen Huang is a CCM intern and UIC student. This week she reports from Chinatown’s Lunar New Year Parade, exploring what the celebration means to the community in the Year of the Horse.

Anne Delfosse

DateBook

Anne Delfosse curates Classic Chicago’s DateBook, the essential guide to Chicago’s social and cultural season—galas, concerts, lectures, and celebrations across the city and suburbs.
