# Chicago, Pinball Capital of the World

**Category:** Culture / Chicago Industry
**Angle:** Nearly every pinball machine ever made came out of Chicago, and one factory still builds them here.
**Timeliness:** Evergreen. Strong photos make it a good cover candidate.
