# From the Trading Pit to the Screen

**Category:** Chicago History / Business
**Angle:** The loudest rooms in Chicago went quiet. What was gained and lost when trading moved from the pits to computers.
**Timeliness:** Evergreen. It could run alongside a Board of Trade Building architecture feature.

## 1. Hero photo
- The trading floor at the height of open outcry: colored jackets and hand signals.
- Caption: CME Group archive or a news photo, credited.

## 2. Lede
- Hook: for more than a century, the prices of the world's grain were set by people shouting at each other on LaSalle Street.
- Narrow to the smaller story: one former trader's last day in the pit.

## 3. Scene
- The pit: jackets, badges, hand signals, noise, and the Ceres statue above.

## 4. The central moment
- The former trader describes the day the pit closed. Quote them verbatim.

## 5. Why it matters
- The human judgment and relationships the pits carried, and the speed and access electronic trading brought.

## 6. Secondary beats
