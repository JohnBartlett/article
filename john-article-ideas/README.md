# John Bartlett: Article Ideas

Ten article ideas for Classic Chicago Magazine that tie Chicago's culture and history to a technologist's point of view. Each idea has its own outline file in this folder.

## The ideas

1. [Behind the Sunday Edition](01-behind-the-sunday-edition.md): how a contributor's email becomes Sunday's magazine.
2. [The First Cell Phone Call Was Made at Soldier Field](02-first-cell-phone-call.md): Ameritech's 1983 launch of the first U.S. commercial cellular network.
3. ["Space Command": Zenith and the Invention of the TV Remote](03-zenith-space-command.md): Robert Adler and the West Side company that changed the living room.
4. [Chicago, Pinball Capital of the World](04-pinball-capital.md): Williams, Bally, Gottlieb, and Stern, still building in Elk Grove Village.
5. [From the Trading Pit to the Screen](05-trading-pit-to-screen.md): the end of open outcry at the Board of Trade and the Merc.
6. [Under the Stands at Stagg Field](06-stagg-field-to-aurora.md): from Chicago Pile-1 to Argonne's supercomputers.
7. [Unsung Gems: the Museum of Science and Industry's Forgotten Exhibits](07-msi-unsung-gems.md): the quieter technology displays.
8. [Forty Years of Code: A Chicago Engineer Looks Back](08-forty-years-of-code.md): a personal essay.
9. [AI Comes to the Arts](09-ai-comes-to-the-arts.md): how Chicago's art schools, galleries, and writers are responding.
10. [Keeping a Magazine Honest in the Age of Machines](10-keeping-a-magazine-honest.md): verbatim text, credits, and the checks behind them.

Before starting any of these, check for overlap with published pieces, especially `chicago-innovation`, `this-day-in-history`, `today-in-chicago-history`, `hawthorne-works`, and the `train-history` series.

## The structure

Every outline uses the same eight-part structure, taken from "Don't Lose That Joy" (July 26, 2026):

1. **Hero photo** with a sourced caption.
2. **Lede.** Start with the news hook, then narrow to the smaller story that's worth telling.
3. **Scene.** Who was in the room and what was happening.
4. **The central moment**, told through verbatim quotes.
5. **Why it matters.** One interpretive paragraph.
6. **Secondary beats.** Lighter or supporting moments.
7. **The Chicago turn.** Connect the story to the city's character.
8. **Close.** The subject gets the last word, in their own words.

Photos go between the beats, and every one needs a sourced caption.

## Before publishing any of these

- Every fact in the "To verify" lists came from memory, not research. Confirm each one against a primary source before it goes into the text.
- Quotes must be verbatim from an interview, a recording, or a cited source. Never paraphrase a quote and put it in quotation marks.
- Keep this folder on dev2. It's internal planning and should never be promoted to dev or master.
